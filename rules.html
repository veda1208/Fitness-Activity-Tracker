RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. Accurate Information – Enter correct information about your workouts, steps, and daily goals.

2. Set Realistic Goals – Set achievable fitness goals according to your age and ability.

3. Regular Tracking – Update your activity and workout details regularly.

4. Stay Hydrated – Drink enough water during and after physical activity.

5. Take Rest – Allow sufficient rest and recovery between workouts.

6. Use Reminders Responsibly – Set reminders that fit your daily routine.

7. Do Not Overexercise – Stop exercising if you feel unwell, dizzy, or unusually tired and tell a trusted adult or seek medical help if needed.

8. Privacy & Security – Keep your login credentials private and do not share your password with others.

9. Data Accuracy – Progress graphs and statistics are based on the information entered or recorded by the app.

10. Not Medical Advice – The app is for fitness tracking and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
